
# Empório da Música — Customer Service Policies

Source: Store Policies and Procedures Manual, version 2.1 (June 2025).

This document contains customer-facing information summarized from the store's reference manual.

## Store Information

- Location: Rua 14 de Maio, 3200, Centro, Campo Grande, MS.
- Monday to Friday: 09:00–18:00.
- Saturday: 09:00–13:00.
- Sundays and public holidays: Closed.
- The store sells musical instruments, not accessories.

## Payment Methods

- PIX: 5% discount on regular prices.
- Debit card: Accepted.
- Credit card: Up to 12 interest-free installments, subject to installment rules.
- Bank slip: Payment confirmation may take up to 3 business days.
- Promotional discounts cannot be combined with the PIX discount.

## Returns and Exchanges

- Online purchases: Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days after delivery.
- The product must be unused, with original packaging and included items.
- Refunds use the original payment method and may take up to 10 business days.
- Return shipping for online purchase withdrawal is paid by the store.
- Exchanges for manufacturing defects may be requested within 30 calendar days of purchase.
- Preference exchanges may be requested within 7 days, subject to product condition and availability.
- Some customized and explicitly final-sale products are not eligible for preference exchanges.

## Delivery

- Campo Grande metropolitan area: Free delivery for orders above R$ 500.
- Orders below R$ 500: R$ 35 delivery fee.
- Estimated local delivery: 1–3 business days.
- Other locations: PAC, SEDEX or Jadlog, depending on the shipment.
- Delivery costs outside the metropolitan area depend on destination and package characteristics.
- Tracking information is provided after dispatch.

## Warranty

- Legal warranty: 90 days against manufacturing defects.
- Additional manufacturer warranties depend on the product.
- Misuse, unauthorized modifications and accidental damage are not covered.

## Customer Support Rules

- Use a friendly, informal but professional tone.
- Never invent product prices, stock levels or delivery dates.
- Verify order details before providing order-specific information.
- Protect customer personal information.
- Escalate complaints and uncertain policy cases to a human attendant.
- Never claim that a refund, reservation or order change has been completed without confirmation.
